03 · Partnering with an FDE03 / 07

A strong partnership, built to deliver shared impact.

The Deployment Strategist job description asks for discovery, scoping, prototyping, program management and a product feedback loop. The Forward Deployed Engineer postings ask for building, integrating, tuning prompts, voices and transcribers, and improving from customer feedback.[7][8] So I'd split the lead by stage, and share the result.

Clearing the way

  • Customer updates handled before they reach the build
  • Escalations and scope changes managed up front
  • Customer context shared, so nothing is rediscovered

Building side by side

  • Prototypes shaped with the FDE from the first sketch
  • Test cases from real calls, shared before the build starts
  • Their code stays theirs; I help where they ask

Staying in sync

  • A weekly 30-minute check-in on one shared page
  • A quick debrief together after every customer session
  • One scorecard we own and review together

04 · A worked example04 / 07

A first telecom customer, served from Madrid.

I picked a hypothetical Spanish telecom operator, an unnamed archetype with several million mobile and fibre subscribers across more than one brand. Three reasons:

  • It's named in the Series C, and no telecom customer has been announced yet.[1]
  • It runs on the same model as energy, where HappyRobot is already live with Naturgy: subscribers, billing, outages, technicians, churn.[9]
  • Spain set a deadline. Under Ley 10/2025, 95% of calls must be answered within three minutes, customers can ask for a human, outage lines must run 24/7, and companies must comply by 28 December 2026.[10][11][12]

06 · My first 30 days06 / 07

Learn the business. Shadow. Earn trust.

I onboard the way I'd deploy: agree on a baseline, act, then measure before moving on.

Monday check-inAgree the one or two highest-impact goals for the week and write down the baseline.
→
ActLearn, shadow, build or propose, depending on the week.
→
Friday measureImpact made, coverage gained, and whether to move on or go deeper.
Week 1
Learn the business, the platform and the people
  • Case studies, pricing models, how a deal reaches deployment
  • Build a first agent workflow on the platform
  • Coffee with someone in every function: "What could I take off your plate?"
  • Rebuild one deployment's economics from scratch, then compare
Week 2
Shadow live deployments
  • Shadow two FDE–strategist pairs at different stages
  • Ask each FDE how they like to work
  • Listen to 50+ real calls and tag why each escalated, checked by an FDE
  • Spend time with the customer's front-line agents
Week 3
Build alongside
  • Own one small, real task through to measurement
  • Rebuild a published use case and run it through tests first
  • Draft a discovery guide and ask a strategist to mark it up
  • Celebrate a teammate's ship out loud, specifically
Week 4
Synthesize and give back
  • Put the telecom thinking in front of the team to be torn apart
  • Co-lead one discovery session
  • Hand over a "first 30 days" doc and the call-pattern library for the next hire
  • Offer an AI-literacy session for non-technical teammates
